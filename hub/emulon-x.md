A sampler in the spirit of E-mu's Emulator X, while also easy to use. Load your own samples or banks, shape them with filters, envelopes and effects, and play them right in your DAW.

## Features
- Multi-zone sampling with auto-mapping
- Splicer: chop a loop into slices, one per key
- Reshape the waveform itself (warp), loops that play forward, backward or ping-pong
- 16 macros, ready to automate
- Shimmer, reverb, delay & more effects
- Arpeggiator
- 7 skins, plus your own

## What's new
-**EMULON X V3 - BETA "Emulation" update**
- Emulator Xs .exb files can now be read (engine tweaks still in progress.)
- added the EMU Engine, making more complicated patches possible, with the Srtyle of the Emulator X (Work in progress - future builds might break current presets that use the EMU engine.)
- new layout, including a search bar for the preset explorer.
- new FX: Spectrum.
- new Mode: Machine. use it like a drum machine, customizable pads. choice between classic 4x4 pads, or 8x8 pads.
- new mode: Arranger. similar to a multimode. activate this, and you will be able to play multiple presets, in the same instance, via different MIDI channels in your DAW.
- Better Keymapping controls, adding a Kontakt-like "overlap" view, where the Zones overlap on each other.
- new envelope modes: Random, and steps (arp-like).
- added a fixed keyboard on the bottom, including a itch wheel, mod wheel, and octave changer buttons.
- **2.0.0ea   SuperSampler (early access)**
- A new Editor: SAMPLE, VOICE and KEYMAP panels you can fold away, and the page scrolls.
- Loop direction: forward, ping-pong or backward.
- Warp: reshape the waveform itself (bend, squeeze, fold, mirror, steps, drive), see it in yellow before you apply it, undo it any time.
- Splicer mode: chop one sample into slices (find hits or split evenly), one slice per key, then keep them as zones if you like.
- A much bigger keymap with a colour for every zone (taken from your skin).
- The stereo delay's repeats now stay exactly on the beat.
- **1.1.1ea   Early access**
- New skins: EMURUM, EMUTAL, EMUBLOX and EMUL8R, picked from a dropdown.
- Banks and presets sort like Windows File Explorer.
- Note names: choose whether middle C is C3 or C4.
- Copy an envelope from another zone; double-click values to type them.
